Welcome to the Just Good Work Data Platform.

The Just Good Work data platform offers information, data and insights about migrant labour. This includes issues that affect migrants from when they are looking for work in their home countries, through their experience migrating and working until they finish. Through this platform, we hope to shed light on the issues that not only affect migrant workers, but also lead to inefficiencies in labour supply chains. This resource is to provide insight and support that will help individuals, organizations, and governments to combat modern slavery and protect the rights of migrant workers.

How can the JGW data platform help you?

Real time insights

Get real time feedbacks and insights from 45000+ workers in over 30 countries.

Track issues specific to your supply chain

By using JGW in your supply chain you can find issues and inefficiencies earlier.

Understand what matters to workers

Improves worker's experience for better attraction, engagement, and retention.

Improve your recruitment and retention

Lower staff turnover and increase retention proactively understanding issues negatively affecting migrants.

Safety for workers

All the information we collect is anonymised to ensure protection for migrants.

Accurate information

We have spent years building trust with migrants and migrant support agencies all over the world, which means we have the relationships to get accurate information
